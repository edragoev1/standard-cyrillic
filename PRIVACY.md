# Privacy

Standard Cyrillic collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.

It reads nothing from the pages you visit but the fonts they use, to change
the shapes of their Cyrillic letters on your screen. It keeps one thing, in
your browser's own extension storage: the list of sites where you turned it
off. That list is not sent to anyone. The extension makes no network
requests; its code and its fonts are all in the package. It has no account,
no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
